## What is agape.sovereign?

agape.sovereign is a zero-knowledge, privacy-first platform for **Digital Identity Federated Footprint (DIFF)** intelligence. It empowers users to scan, analyze, and reclaim control over their digital identity across 16 identity vectors — from email breaches to biometric exposure — using AI analysis and compliance-grade encrypted reporting.

Built entirely on **Firebase free-tier infrastructure** + **Google AI services**, with no paid cloud dependencies.
