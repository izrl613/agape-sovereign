This Privacy Policy describes how Agape Sovereign AI ("the Platform," "we," "us," or "our") handles personal information when you access or use our services at sovereign.nyc.

1. Our Privacy Philosophy & Zero-Knowledge Architecture

Agape Sovereign AI is a privacy-first, security-intelligence platform designed to help users map, audit, and remediate exposures across their Digital Identity Federated Footprint (DIFF). We operate under a client-side zero-knowledge security model:

  • Sensitive identity telemetry is encrypted client-side using AES-GCM 256-bit encryption before leaving your device.
  • We do not sell, broker, license, or monetize your identity data.
  • No third-party behavioral profiling or commercial advertising tracking is used.
  • Platform administrators have access only to anonymized operational diagnostics, never raw identity information.

3. Information We Collect & Google User Data

3.1 Google OAuth 2.0 Authentication Data

When you authenticate using Google Sign-In, we receive basic identity profile information from Google:

  • Your primary email address (used strictly for account identification and authentication).
  • Your display name and profile picture URL (used for in-app profile presentation).
  • A unique provider-issued user identifier (UID) created by Firebase Authentication.

This authentication data is isolated and never combined with or correlated to external data brokers.

3.2 Google OAuth Scopes Requested

The Platform requests only the minimum necessary scopes to deliver user-requested features:

  • openid — Confirms user identity with Google.
  • email — Retrieves user email address for authentication and notification.
  • profile — Retrieves basic user profile details (name and profile image).
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file — (Optional / User-Initiated only): If and only if you explicitly choose the "Export to Google Drive" feature for identity audit reports, the Platform requests this scope to create a dedicated "Agape Sovereign" folder in your Google Drive and upload your encrypted report. The Platform does not read, access, or modify any other files in your Google Drive.
In Plain English: When signing in with Google, we only receive your email, name, and profile picture. We cannot read your Gmail or access other files in your Drive. If you choose to export an identity report to Drive, we only save that specific PDF into your own Google Drive.

3.3 Google API Services User Data Policy & Limited Use Disclosure

Agape Sovereign's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • No Artificial Intelligence (AI) Model Training: Google user data is never used to develop, improve, or train generalized or non-personalized artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models.
  • No Data Transfer or Sale: Google user data is never sold, licensed, or transferred to third-party data brokers, advertising networks, or data aggregators.
  • Revocation of Access: You can inspect and revoke the Platform's access to your Google account at any time via your Google Account Permissions page.

3.4 WebAuthn FIDO2 Passkey Data

When you bind a Passkey to your device, we store only the public key credential and credential ID on our secure servers. Your biometric data and private cryptographic keys never leave your physical device enclave.

4. How We Use Information

We process information solely to provide, operate, and maintain the Platform's zero-knowledge security services, authenticate your session, generate requested identity security reports, and protect against security abuse.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Instructions

Encrypted data stored in DIFF modules is retained until you delete your account or submit a deletion request. Operational logs are retained for no longer than 90 days.

How to Delete Your Data: You can request complete deletion of your account and all associated telemetry at any time by emailing agape@sovereign.nyc with the subject "Data Deletion Request." Upon receipt, your encrypted records are deleted from Firebase within 30 days.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Significant changes will be posted prominently on the Platform with an updated effective date.
